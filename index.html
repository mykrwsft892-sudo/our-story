Chapter One

How It All Began

September 22, 2026 — the day a beautiful stranger became someone special.

01
22 SEPTEMBER 2026

The Girl I Couldn't Forget

We met in Sharm El Sheikh during the Safari 5-in-1 trip. You were staying at Albatros Golf with your friend Marta. I had met many people before, but there was something different about you.

You were so beautiful, so kind, and there was something almost magical about the way you carried yourself. It felt like you cast a spell on me, and from that moment, I couldn't stop thinking about you.

I didn't know what would happen next. I only knew that I wanted to know you better — even if it was just for one more conversation.

02
A LITTLE DESERT MEMORY

Tea, Bread & A Photo

At the Bedouin tent, we shared tea and fresh Bedouin bread, and I got to take a photo with you. It might have looked like an ordinary moment to anyone else, but for me, it became one of those little memories I wanted to keep forever.

The desert was quiet, the lights were warm, and you were smiling. Sometimes, the smallest moments become the ones we remember the most.

03
THE FUNNY LITTLE MOMENT

The Power Bank & The Shisha

At dinner, a Turkish girl needed a power bank. You asked Marta to give it to me, and when you saw me hand it to the girl, you thought it was mine.

Later, when the shisha seller gave you a price you didn't like, you came to me and asked whether I would give you the shisha or take the power bank!

I told you that wasn't very nice. But when I noticed the regret in your eyes, I didn't want you to feel bad, so I bought you the shisha and gave you money to pay for it.

Then we sat together, talked a little, and I gave you the power bank back. A funny misunderstanding became one of my favorite memories of us.

04
UNDER THE STARS

And Then You Became "Lina"

At the end of the trip, I asked for your Instagram, and you gave it to me. Then we went to look at the stars. The sky was beautiful, but honestly, my thoughts were somewhere else — they were all on you.

I had trouble saying your name, and you told me I could call you "Lina" or "Rouhi" in Arabic. From that night, you weren't just Adelina anymore. You became my Lina.

When it was time for you to leave on the hotel bus, we said goodbye. I didn't know that this was only the beginning of our story.

05
SEPTEMBER 23, 2026

The Surprise You Said Yes To

After midnight, I sent you the photos from the previous day. You asked me to find a good and affordable place for snorkeling. I told you I had a nice surprise: snorkeling and parasailing for only €25.

You agreed right away — but you insisted that I come too. I was sleepy, but how could I say no to you? You even kept calling me in the morning to wake me up, because you knew I wouldn't get up on my own.

We met near Pizza Hut, went snorkeling and parasailing, and made a whole new set of memories. I was happy that you wanted me there with you. That day meant more to me than you probably know.

06
A MEMORY THAT MAKES ME SMILE

Your Two Injured Feet 😂❤️

When you came out of the water, coral had cut your foot. I helped you sit down and asked my friend to get some treatment. I treated your foot and tried to help you feel better.

Then, just three minutes later, I discovered that your other foot was injured too! So, of course, I had to take care of that one as well. I still smile every time I remember that little adventure.

Afterward, we got ice cream, and I took you back to your hotel. I was glad I could help you, even in such a small way.

07
THE LAST HUG IN SHARM

A Goodbye I Didn't Want

Before you left for your flight to Spain, I hugged Marta, then hugged you and kissed your forehead. I knew you were leaving that night, and I didn't know when I would see you again.

That hug became our last goodbye in Egypt. But even after you left, our story didn't end. You went back to Spain, and I stayed in Egypt. Somehow, we kept talking, sharing our days, and getting closer despite the distance.

And every day since then, I've realized more and more how much you mean to me.

"I didn't plan to meet you. I didn't plan to care this much. But I'm really glad that I did."

— Omar, to Adelina ❤️

Distance Is Not The Whole Story

But I want to learn, to understand you better, and to keep showing you through my actions that you matter to me. I don't expect everything to be easy. I just hope that, if we both want this, we can face the difficulties together — one day at a time.

Distance is just a number. What matters is what we choose to do with it.

Maybe one day, we will travel together, laugh about our memories, support each other through difficult days, and celebrate every little victory side by side.

I dream of a future where we can build a home full of love, respect, laughter, and peace. A future where we can face the world together and make each other feel safe and understood.

And sometimes, when I imagine that future, I imagine two little names too: Noah and Sarah.

But I know a dream is only a dream until two people choose it together. I don't want to decide our future for you. I want to discover it with you, if your heart wants the same thing.
